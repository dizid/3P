## The Problem

People face important decisions — career moves, business pivots, major purchases — and either:
1. Overthink endlessly (analysis paralysis)
2. Ask ChatGPT and get a generic wall of text
3. Make a gut call and hope for the best

None of these close the loop. Nobody tracks whether their decision process actually worked.

## The Solution

3PS provides **8 proven decision frameworks** (10-10-10 Rule, Regret Minimization, PMI, SWOT, etc.) with structured scoring, AI coaching, and — uniquely — **outcome tracking that tells you how good your decisions actually are over time**.

## Key Differentiators

### 1. Outcome Tracking Loop (No competitor does this)
Most decision tools help you decide. None help you learn from your decisions.
- Save decision → 30 days later → "How did it go?" email
- Rate outcome 1-10 → builds your personal accuracy score
- Over time: "You make better decisions with SWOT than PMI" — personalized insight

### 2. AI Coaching Conversation (Phase 2)
Not just a summary — a conversation. The AI challenges your thinking:
- "You rated financial security 80 but scored this project's money impact at 40. What changed?"
- "Your fear of inaction outweighs fear of action 3:1. Is that typical for you?"
- Socratic method, 5 follow-ups per decision

### 3. Multi-Framework Comparison
No other tool lets you run the same decision through multiple frameworks and compare:
- "10-10-10 says go, Regret Minimization says go, but SWOT is negative — here's why..."
- AI synthesizes conflicts across frameworks

### 4. Purpose-Built vs Generic AI
ChatGPT is a blank canvas. 3PS is a specialized instrument:
- Proven frameworks with validated scoring formulas
- Consistent output format every time
- History and patterns that compound over months
